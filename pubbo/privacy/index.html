Privacy policy

Pubbo · Effective November 13, 2026

Pubbo is an Android EPUB reader developed by Zhengping Jiang. This policy explains the information used by the app and its optional connected features. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to tony.jiang.zhengping@gmail.com.

Local reading

Pubbo reads the EPUB files you select and stores imported books, covers, reading positions, preferences, bookmarks, highlights, and saved overview content on your device. You can read without signing in. Local reading alone does not upload your books to Pubbo’s cloud service.

Google sign-in and account information

If you sign in with Google, Pubbo receives an account identifier, email address, and available profile information such as your name and profile picture. Google authenticates you; Pubbo does not receive your Google password. Supabase processes authentication and stores the Pubbo account profile. Authentication credentials are stored on the device using Android Keystore-backed protection.

We use account information to sign you in, restore your session, associate cloud data with your account, and respond to support or deletion requests. Pubbo does not request access to your Gmail, Google Drive files, or contacts through this sign-in flow.

Cloud synchronization

For eligible accounts with cloud access, signing in starts synchronization automatically. Cloud data can include EPUB files and covers; titles, authors, and other book metadata; reading positions; bookmarks; highlights and their text; saved page excerpts; generated summaries; and related reading settings. This information lets you use your library across devices.

Supabase hosts account and synchronization records. Cloudflare Workers handles service requests, and Cloudflare R2 stores cloud files. These providers process data to deliver the service. Local reading remains available without cloud access.

Optional ChatGPT features

Connecting ChatGPT is separate from signing in with Google. If you connect it and use explanations or summaries, Pubbo sends relevant selected passages, surrounding context, book text needed for the requested summary, and generation instructions to OpenAI. A summary can require multiple portions of a book. Results may be cached on your device and saved with your reading data; eligible cloud accounts may synchronize saved overview content.

OpenAI handles these requests under the terms and privacy settings applicable to your connected account. Pubbo cannot promise how an independent provider retains data or uses it. You can disconnect ChatGPT in the app and manage its authorization in your ChatGPT account. See OpenAI’s privacy policy.

Read aloud, service operation, and support

Read-aloud passes book text to the Android text-to-speech engine you choose. That engine may process text locally or through its provider’s network service, depending on the voice and your settings.

When you use online features, service providers receive network information such as your IP address, request time, and technical request details. Operational and error records help maintain the service and investigate failures or abuse. If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any information you choose to include. Do not send passwords, access tokens, or entire books when requesting help.

Payments and advertising

Paid cloud checkout is not enabled for the initial test release. If subscriptions become available, Google Play will process payments; Pubbo’s service will use purchase tokens and subscription status to verify cloud access. Pubbo does not receive full payment-card details. Pubbo does not sell personal information or use advertising SDKs to target ads.

Retention and deletion

Local library data stays on your device until you remove it or clear the app’s storage. Signing out or deleting a Pubbo account does not erase your local books. Remove downloaded copies on each device separately if you no longer want them.

Account and synchronized library data are retained while needed to provide your account and cloud library, until you remove the relevant data or delete your account. Account deletion removes the account profile and associated cloud library data. Security and operational records may be retained separately where needed to investigate abuse or meet legal obligations; service-provider backups and records follow the providers’ retention processes. We do not promise that deletion instantly removes every backup copy.

You can request deletion in Pubbo’s Account screen or through the account-deletion page, including if you no longer have the app. Email requests may require verification that you control the account. We will confirm completion or explain any remaining retention and its reason. Pubbo account deletion does not delete your Google or OpenAI account, remove information independently held by those services, or cancel a Google Play subscription.

Security and your choices

Online service requests use HTTPS, cloud access is associated with your account, and saved device credentials use platform protection. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. You can choose local reading, sign out, disconnect ChatGPT, delete local books, or request account deletion. Contact us to ask about access to or correction of your account information.

This website and policy updates

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical visit information to operate its hosting service; see GitHub’s privacy statement. These Pubbo pages do not add analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts.

We will update this page when data handling changes and revise the effective date. Material changes will be explained before introducing the affected features.
