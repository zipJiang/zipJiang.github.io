Delete your Pubbo account

You can request deletion in the app or by email, even if you no longer have Pubbo installed.

In Pubbo

Open the Account screen, choose Delete account, and confirm the deletion. This removes your Pubbo account and associated cloud library data.

Without the app

Email tony.jiang.zhengping@gmail.com from the Google email address you used to sign in to Pubbo. Use the subject Delete my Pubbo account and say that you want your account and associated cloud data deleted.

Start a deletion request by email

If you cannot send from that address, tell us which account the request concerns. We may ask for enough information to verify account ownership. Do not send your password, a sign-in code, or an access token. We will confirm completion or explain any follow-up needed.

What is removed

The request covers your Pubbo account profile and associated synchronized books, covers, reading positions, bookmarks, highlights, and saved overview data. See the privacy policy for retention details, including service-provider records and backups.

What you manage separately

  • Local copies: account deletion does not erase books or reading data stored on your devices. Delete them in the app or clear its storage on each device.
  • Google Play subscriptions: if you have an active subscription, cancel it in Google Play’s subscription settings. You do not need to cancel it before requesting account deletion.
  • Google and ChatGPT accounts: these are independent accounts. Manage their data and Pubbo’s authorizations with the respective provider.
