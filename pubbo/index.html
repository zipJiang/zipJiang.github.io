Pubbo is an EPUB reader for Android, built around reading, highlighting, and finding your place again.

Pubbo is currently in testing. It is not yet publicly available on Google Play.

Read locally

Open EPUB files you choose from your device. Adjust typography, save bookmarks and highlights, and pick up where you left off. Local reading does not require an account.

Optional connected features

Sign in with Google to create a Pubbo account. Accounts with cloud access can synchronize their library and reading data. Connecting ChatGPT separately enables supported explanations and summaries, which send the relevant book text to OpenAI.

Feature availability may vary during testing. Paid cloud subscriptions are not being offered through this test release.
