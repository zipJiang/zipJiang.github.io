Alongside my research, I've been building a small side project: an EPUB reader for Android called Pubbo. It's still in testing, but it's far enough along that I wanted to write a little about it.

What I wanted was a reader that stays out of the way. You open a book, read, mark the passages that matter, and when you come back later you're right where you left off. That's most of what Pubbo tries to do.

So the basics all live on the phone. You pick EPUB files from your device, set the typography the way you like it, and Pubbo keeps your bookmarks, highlights, and reading position. None of that needs an account.

There are a couple of extras if you want them. Signing in with Google lets your library and reading data sync across devices. You can also connect ChatGPT and ask for an explanation or a summary of the passage you're on. When you do that, the relevant book text is sent to OpenAI, so I've kept it strictly opt-in.

Pubbo isn't on Google Play yet. When it is, I'll say so here. In the meantime, the [privacy policy](/pubbo/privacy/) and [terms of use](/pubbo/terms/) are already up, and the [Pubbo page](/pubbo/) has the rest of the details.
